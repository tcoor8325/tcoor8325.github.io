# AGENTS

This folder is a playful corner of the portfolio: tiny, vibe-coded experiments and single-braincell ideas. Keep things simple, fast, and fun.

## Scope
- This folder hosts small, self-contained "dumb apps" and micro-experiments.
- Each app should be easy to open and understand in under a minute.

## Non-Goals
- No heavy frameworks or complex build systems unless explicitly requested.
- No long-lived services, backends, or databases.

## Conventions
- Prefer plain HTML/CSS/JS per app.
- Keep assets local and small.
- Favor clarity and punchy humor over completeness.
- Keep code compact but readable.
- Accessibility basics still matter (contrast, keyboard focus, alt text).

## Suggested Structure (if/when apps are added)
- `apps/<slug>/index.html`
- `apps/<slug>/style.css`
- `apps/<slug>/app.js`
- `apps/<slug>/README.md` (1-paragraph explanation)

## Review Bar
- Open in a browser with no build step.
- Each app should load in under a second and feel like a joke that landed.

## When In Doubt
- Choose the simpler path.
- Ship the silly thing.
