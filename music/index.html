About the Songbook

This page is a collection of all of my favorite folk and folksy songs that are not yet in the Rise Up Singing or Rise Again songbook catalogs! This is an attempt to adapt these songs for the special format and notation of those books, and to set them together into a new booklet with LaTeX. Almost all of these songs had entries on ultimate-guitar.com, which I've tried to adapt. Actually I've gone through a few iterations now of adapting them, and they pretty much all still need work! But feel free to browse.
