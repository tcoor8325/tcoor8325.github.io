About

Thomas Coor is an electrical engineer and designer from New York City. His hobby of musical instrument design lead him to a career in Audio and Acoustic Engineering and Manufacturing. His work in the field showed him how exactly consumer products are designed and manufactured, and he discovered how much is wasted in creating non-repairable and bad products. He now wants to transition to designing sustainable and environmentally-minded devices for Humanity.
