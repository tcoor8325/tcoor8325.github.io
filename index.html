About

Thomas Coor is an electrical engineer and designer from Oyster Bay on Long Island, outside of New York City. Growing up in the rich ecological region instilled a love of the natural world and a desire to preserve it. Efforts to improve his bassoon reeds in high school introduced him to the world of acoustics, and he studied electrical engineering at the Cooper Union in New York City to explore this fascination further. There he built many musical instruments and was a finalist in the Guthman Musical Instrument Design Competition. Since graduating, Thomas has worked in Audio Electronics manufacturing, learning about how devices are designed for modern production. It has fiercely reignited his interest in ecology. Thomas isn't sure what to make of this just yet, but he is considering how to steer his career based on his feelings.
