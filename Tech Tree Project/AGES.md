# Ages

- **Early Foraging Age** (c. 3,300,000-200,000 BCE): First stone tools, controlled fire, early hominin spread.
- **Late Foraging Age** (c. 200,000-10,000 BCE): Behaviorally modern humans, complex symbolic culture, wide trade in prestige goods.
- **Early Farming Age** (c. 10,000-4000 BCE): First domestications, permanent villages, basic field systems and herding.
- **Urban Agrarian Age** (c. 4000-1000 BCE): Cities, writing, early states, large irrigation works, bronze and early iron.
- **Imperial Agrarian Age** (c. 1000 BCE-500 CE): Large empires on multiple continents, codified law, major religious and philosophical traditions, iron widespread.
- **Connected Agrarian Age** (c. 500-1500 CE): Dense long-distance trade and knowledge exchange across regions; improved ships, gunpowder and advanced agronomy in many parts of the world.
- **Mechanized Production Age** (c. 1500-1800 CE): Clockwork, improved water and wind power, early factories and workshops, more systematic science and engineering.
- **Fossil-Industrial Age** (c. 1800-1970 CE): Steam power, railways, large factories, steel, modern chemistry, telegraph and telephone, mass schooling, mass media.
- **Information Age** (c. 1970 CE-present): Computers, digital communication, satellites, integrated global logistics, automation, AI and data-centric systems.
